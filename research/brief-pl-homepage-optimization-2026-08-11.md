## Executive Summary

**Polish-language B2B charger/power bank OEM homepage has zero competition.** Three rounds of SERP research confirm: no Polish manufacturer, trading company, or importer has a Polish-language B2B OEM homepage. The only ranking results for "producent OEM power bank Chiny" are WOWOHCOOL's own EN pages, Chinese B2B directories (Alibaba, Made-in-China), and a single Polish consumer brand (Green Cell — consumer, not OEM).

WOWOHCOOL's PL homepage has first-mover advantage: **the only Polish-language OEM manufacturer homepage in this niche.** Optimization should focus on title/meta tuning, trust signal amplification, and competitor differentiation — not structural rewrite.
