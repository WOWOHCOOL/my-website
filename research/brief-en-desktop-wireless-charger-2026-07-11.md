### Volume & Revenue
- Desktop = **55-60% of wireless charger unit sales** — the volume leader
- Global production: ~25M all-in-one units (2025), growing ~7% annually
- ASP: ~$39.60/unit (all-in-one), ~$18/unit (basic stands)
- Gross margins: 25-40%

### Qi2 Adoption
- 40-50% of MPP-compatible devices in Western markets by 2026
- Amazon & Best Buy de-listing non-Qi2 products (2025-2026)
- 5W Qi1 exiting Western mainstream by 2026-2027
- Qi2 products carry 15-30% price premium over Qi1

### Corporate Procurement
- Direct corporate procurement: 8-12% of unit sales, growing
- 42% of European employees hybrid/remote in 2026
- Furniture retailers bundling wireless charging with office packages
- Enterprise gifting: "premium look + dependable performance" demand driver
