## 3. Current Page Assessment

### Strengths (Keep)
- **Schema markup**: ManufacturingBusiness + 2 Person schemas + AboutPage + BreadcrumbList + FAQPage + WebSite — comprehensive
- **Visual design**: Hero with counter animations, factory photos, team cards with photos and LinkedIn links
- **Social proof**: Bosch + Jacob Jensen case studies embedded
- **Certification showcase**: Blue banner with cert icons
- **FAQ section**: 8 Q&As with schema markup
- **Trust bar + customer logo carousel**: Included via partials
- **CTA buttons**: Schedule Factory Tour + Contact Sales, both above the fold

### Weaknesses (Optimize)
1. **Content depth too thin**: ~800 words total. Competitive about pages range 1,200-2,500 words. Content is mostly in image alt text and bullet points, not in searchable paragraphs.
2. **Missing company history timeline**: "Since 2013" badge exists but no narrative about growth trajectory, milestones, CES 2026.
3. **Factory section is weak on text**: Heavy on images and icon cards, light on descriptive content about actual manufacturing capabilities.
4. **Team bios are brief**: 2-3 sentence descriptions. Could expand with specific achievements, years at company, languages spoken.
5. **No "Why Choose Us" comparison**: Competitors list specs; no one frames it as a decision matrix.
6. **Missing downloadable assets**: Company profile PDF, factory brochure, capability deck — no gated content to capture leads.
7. **Breadcrumb in hero uses text "About Us" but H1 format doesn't match blog article style**
8. **No embedded video**: "Schedule Factory Tour" CTA exists but a short factory walkthrough GIF/video would build instant trust.
