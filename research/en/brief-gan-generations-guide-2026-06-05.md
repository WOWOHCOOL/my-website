## 7. FAQ Schema 扩展（5 → 10 条）

**保留 5 条**（已有）+ **新增 5 条**：

6. **What's the difference between enhancement-mode and cascode GaN?**
   "Enhancement-mode (e-mode) GaN is used in GaN III/V and switches natively without a silicon MOSFET. Cascode GaN combines a silicon MOSFET with a depletion-mode GaN HEMT — used in GaN I. E-mode reduces switching losses and PCB complexity but requires careful gate driving."

7. **Why do charger specs skip GaN II and GaN IV?**
   "GaN II and GaN IV exist primarily in research and high-voltage industrial applications such as LiDAR, satellite power, and EV traction. They have not entered consumer charger BOMs in commercial volume."

8. **What GaN FET part numbers should I look for in OEM quotes?**
   "For GaN V, request Navitas NV6128, Innoscience INN700 series, or Power Integrations InnoSwitch 4-CZ. For GaN III, NV6115 or GaN Systems GS66508T are common. Always cross-reference the part number against the manufacturer's published datasheet."

9. **Does GaN V support all PD 3.1 EPR voltage steps?**
   "Yes. GaN V's 5MHz+ switching enables clean transitions across 28V, 36V, and 48V EPR levels, plus PPS 20mV granularity. GaN III typically caps at 21V PD 3.0 SPR."

10. **When will GaN VI hit the market?**
    "Industry roadmaps from Navitas and Innoscience suggest GaN VI engineering samples in 2027-2028, with commercial volume around 2029. Expect integrated digital control and improved gate-drive efficiency."
