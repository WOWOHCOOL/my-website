|---------|---------|-----|------------|--------------|-------------|--------|

| Charger w/ data transfer | 8517.62 | 0% | 25% | 10% | ~35% | CBP Ruling N360577 (Apr 2026) |

*Section 122 (10% global) struck down by CIT for plaintiffs only (May 2026); for non-plaintiffs, it remains payable through July 24, 2026 unless Congress extends. Refund eligibility uncertain.

### Pre-Feb 20, 2026 Stack (for refund context)
- IEEPA "reciprocal" tariff on China: **54%** — STRUCK DOWN, refunds available via PSC or protest within 180 days of liquidation.
- Plus Section 301 (still in force).
- Plus MFN.

### EU Duty Rates (post-2026, no major changes)
- HS 8504.40: 0% MFN + VAT 19-27% by member state
- HS 8507.60 (Li-ion battery): 2.7% MFN + VAT 19-27%
- HS 8543.70: 3.7% MFN + VAT 19-27%
- **NEW for 2026**: EU Battery Regulation 2026 compliance required (passport, recycled content reporting, due diligence).

### UK Duty Rates
- HS 8504.40: 0% + VAT 20%
- HS 8507.60: 0% (post-Brexit reduction) + VAT 20%
- HS 8543.70: 0% + VAT 20%

### Fixed US Customs Fees (2026)
- **MPF (Merchandise Processing Fee)**: 0.3464% of cargo value, **min $32.71 / max $634.62** per entry
- **HMF (Harbor Maintenance Fee)**: 0.125% of cargo value (ocean only)
- **Customs broker**: $150-500 per shipment ($200-350 recurring)
- **ISF filing**: $25-50 per shipment
- **Single-entry bond**: ~0.5% of duties, min $50
- **Continuous bond**: $500-600/year, unlimited entries
