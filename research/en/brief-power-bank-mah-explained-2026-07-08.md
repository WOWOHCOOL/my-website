## 3. Key Data (from existing brief + factory-data-panel.md)

### mAh Capacity Facts
| Concept | Value |
|---------|-------|
| Battery nominal voltage | 3.7V (Li-Polymer / Li-ion) |
| USB output voltage | 5V (standard) |
| Voltage conversion loss | 15-20% |
| Real-world usable output | **55-70% of rated mAh** |
| Premium circuit efficiency | 85%+ |
| Airline carry-on limit | 100Wh = ~27,000mAh at 3.7V |
| FAA rule | Under 100Wh: no approval. Over 100Wh: airline approval required |
| NEVER in checked baggage | FAA, IATA, CAA — all agree |

### Usable Capacity Formula
```
Usable charges = (Rated mAh × 3.7V ÷ 5V × 0.85) ÷ Device battery mAh

Example: 10,000mAh bank charging iPhone with 3,200mAh battery
= (10,000 × 3.7 ÷ 5 × 0.85) ÷ 3,200
= 6,290 ÷ 3,200
= ~2 full charges
```
