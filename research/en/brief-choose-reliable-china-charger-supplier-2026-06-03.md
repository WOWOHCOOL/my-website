## 3. Content Gaps & Opportunities

### Gap 1: Missing QC Metrics & Benchmark Table
**Why it matters**: Buyers evaluating suppliers need numerical benchmarks to compare. "Our QC is good" is meaningless. "98% yield, <0.5% defect rate, 4-hour 100% aging test" is a spec they can verify.

**Recommendation**: Add a dedicated "Quality Benchmarks" subsection under Section 5 (Quality Control) with a table:

| Metric | WOWOHCOCOL Standard | Industry Minimum |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|
| Yield Rate | ≥99% | ≥98% |
| Defect Rate | <0.3% | <0.5% |
| Aging Test | 4-hour full-load, 100% of units | 2-hour sample-based |
| On-Time Delivery | ≥98% | ≥95% |
| Warranty | 24 months | 12 months |

### Gap 2: Missing Supplier Evaluation Scorecard
**Why it matters**: Commercial-intent buyers want a tool. A downloadable or printable scorecard transforms the article from "read this" to "use this."

**Recommendation**: Add a "Quick Supplier Scorecard" section before the FAQ:

| Criteria | Weight | Score (1-5) |
|----------|--------|-------------|
| ISO 9001 + certs verified on official databases | High | /5 |
| Factory audit (video or in-person) passed | High | /5 |
| Sample quality matches production claims | High | /5 |
| On-time delivery history ≥95% | Medium | /5 |
| OEM/ODM customization capability | Medium | /5 |
| Pricing competitiveness | Low | /5 |

### Gap 3: Missing Certification Verification Guide
**Why it matters**: First-time buyers don't know how to verify if a CE/FCC/UL cert is real. Explaining the verification method builds massive trust and positions WOWOHCOOL as transparent.

**Recommendation**: Add a short subsection under Section 2 (Verify Factory):

| Certification | How to Verify |
|---------------|---------------|
| ISO 9001 | Check cert number on issuing body database (SGS, TÜV, BV) |
| FCC | Look up FCC ID on fcc.gov |
| CE | Verify notifier number + NB certificates |
| UL | Check ul.com database |
| Qi | Verify on wirelesspowerconsortium.com |

### Gap 4: Missing Testing Equipment & Lab Capabilities
**Why it matters**: B2B buyers evaluating factories want to know what testing equipment exists. Lists of equipment (oscilloscopes, thermal imaging, AOI, ESD testers) signal a serious manufacturer.

**Recommendation**: Add 1-2 paragraphs in Section 5 listing in-house testing capabilities and what each test catches.

### Gap 5: Missing FOB Price Benchmarks
**Why it matters**: Buyers need pricing context to evaluate quotes. Without benchmarks, they don't know if a quote is reasonable.

**Recommendation**: Add a small "Price Reference" callout box:

| Product Type | FOB Shenzhen Reference |
|--------------|----------------------|
| 20W PD Charger | $2.50-4.00 |
| 65W GaN Charger | $5.98-8.00 |
| 100W GaN Desktop Charger | $8.00-12.00 |
| 10,000mAh Power Bank | $5.98-11.98 |

Note: "These are reference ranges. Request a formal quote for your specific specs and volume."

### Gap 6: Missing Supply Chain Risk / Geopolitical Context
**Why it matters**: The DOJ's $54.4M customs fraud settlement (Dec 2025) is mentioned in the intro but never revisited. There's an opportunity to connect sourcing decisions to broader trade compliance.

**Recommendation**: Brief subsection on "Trade Compliance in 2026: What Has Changed" — covering forced labor enforcement, tariff classification, and origin documentation.
