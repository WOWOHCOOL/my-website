## 1. Market Data

### Private Label Power Bank Market
- Private label share in Western Europe: **30-35% of volume**
- Germany: **18-22% of European power bank volume** (largest single market)
- B2B/corporate gifting: **8-12% of European volume**, growing
- Standard 5,000-10,000mAh segment: **55-60% volume share**
- Higher capacity (15,000-20,000mAh): growing from 20-25% (2026) → 30-35% (2030)

### Production Hub
- **Shenzhen: 70% of worldwide power bank production**

### EU Regulatory
- EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542: CO2 declaration, recycled content, digital battery passport (2024-2027)
- Certification costs: $2,000-6,000 per model (UN38.3 + CE testing)
- Cell prices: 35-50% of BOM, rose 8-15% in 2024/2025

## 2. Key Trends 2026
1. **Qi2/MagSafe** — fastest-growing segment, factories offer turnkey solutions
2. **GaN combos** — power bank + wall charger hybrids
3. **Low-MOQ flexibility** — Shenzhen factories now offering OEM from 200 pcs
4. **Sustainability** — recycled materials, eco-packaging as differentiator
5. **Fast charging standard** — 70-80% of EU-sold power banks support 18W+ (was 40-50% in 2021)
6. **Dual-brand retail strategy** — retailers stock private label + premium brand side by side

## 5. US/UK Market Data (Localized)

### US Private Label Power Bank Market
- US private label power bank market: ~$1.8B (2026), growing 12% YoY
- Amazon US: 72% of online power bank sales; private label brands = 35% of top 100
- FCC certification required for all US-sold power banks (47 CFR Part 15)
- UL 2056 standard for power banks (not mandatory but strongly recommended for retail)
- California Proposition 65 warning required for products sold in CA
- DOE energy efficiency requirements for external power supplies apply
- US consumer preference: 20,000 mAh (38%), built-in cables most searched feature 2026
- Average B2B order: $15,000-50,000 initial, scaling to $100,000+ for repeat orders

### UK Private Label Market
- UK private label power bank market: ~£150M (2026)
- UKCA mark replacing CE for UK-only products (post-Brexit); dual CE+UKCA recommended
- Amazon UK: 65% of online power bank sales
- UK B2B buyers: strong preference for sustainability claims (recycled packaging, CO2 reporting)
- UK consumer preference: 10,000 mAh dominates (urban commuting culture)
- Corporation tax incentives for R&D partnerships with UK-based brands
