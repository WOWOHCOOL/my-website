## 2. Competitive Landscape

### 竞品共同覆盖（必备）
- ✅ mAh vs Wh
- ✅ Li-polymer / Li-ion 化学
- ✅ PD / QC 协议
- ✅ 航空规则
- ✅ 容量计算
- ✅ 选型决策

### 内容缺口（竞品没讲，我们应放大）
1. **GB47372-2026 中国新国标**：2026/3 发布、2027/4 强制，关于电芯品质、保护电路、UN38.3 升级测试。这是 2026 下半年最大事件。
2. **PD 3.1 EPR 240W**：现阶段进入量产，竞品几乎没拆解 EPR 电压档位（28V/36V/48V）。
3. **半固态电池真实参数**：cycle life、能量密度、热失控温度。竞品多停留在概念层。
4. **认证矩阵**：UN38.3 + CE + FCC + RoHS + UL2056（US power bank 专属）+ PSE + KC，按市场画清单。
5. **采购方常见踩坑**：mAh 虚标（38000mAh 实际 10000mAh 这类陷阱）、电芯掺次品、保护板缩水。

### 差异化策略
- **First-party 工厂**：唯一一篇出自 ISO 9001 工厂、给真实 BOM 与电芯供应商的 specs guide
- **2026 时效**：锚定 GB47372 + PD 3.1 EPR + 半固态量产
- **真实 SKU**：WOP09（半固态）、WOP67（2-in-1）、智能显示款（smart-display 子分类）
- **采购可执行**：每节末附"现在能做的下一步"动作（验收脚本、合规清单）

## 6. Hook 草稿

> A power bank's spec sheet is its truth-teller — but only if you know how to read it. **38,000mAh on the label** can mean 99.9Wh of real capacity, or 35Wh of inflated marketing. A "PD 3.1" label can mean 240W EPR with full laptop support, or 65W stuck at the legacy ceiling.
>
> **Quick answer**: For airline-safe + laptop-class charging in 2026, target **99.9Wh / 27,000mAh / PD 3.1 EPR up to 140W / semi-solid-state cells / UN38.3 + CE + FCC + UL2056 certified**. Anything missing one of these is shipping into a market that's about to tighten.
>
> This guide is written from inside a Shenzhen ISO 9001 power bank factory that ships 1M+ units per month — and is already certified to the 2027 GB47372 standard that will reshape Chinese power bank exports.
