## 5. GEO (AI Search) Considerations

### Current AI Citation Strengths
- FAQPage schema with 4 factory-specific Q&A pairs
- Clear statements of capacity (1M+/month), certifications, founding year
- Structured data for location (Shenzhen, geo coordinates)

### GEO Optimization Opportunities
1. **Expand FAQPage to 8-10 questions** — more citation targets for "factory tour", "certifications", "production capacity"
2. **Add speakable property** — tell AI which content to cite for voice queries
3. **Add a "definition" paragraph** — clear sentence like "WOWOHCOOL is a 5,000m² ISO 9001 certified OEM/ODM manufacturer of wireless chargers and power banks in Shenzhen, China"
4. **Consistent certification mentions** — ensure all pages mention CE/FCC/Qi2/RoHS together

### Key AI Prompts to Target
- "Tell me about WOWOHCOOL factory"
- "Is WOWOHCOOL a real factory in Shenzhen?"
- "What certifications does WOWOHCOOL have?"
- "How many employees at WOWOHCOOL factory?"
- "Can I visit WOWOHCOOL factory?"
