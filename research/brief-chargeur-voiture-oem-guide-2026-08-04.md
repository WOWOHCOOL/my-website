### Gaps & Optimization Opportunities 🔧

#### Gap 1: Fleet TCO Calculator / Cost Analysis (High Priority)
The article mentions fleet cost savings (60-80% cable replacement reduction) but lacks a structured TCO analysis. Fleet managers are the highest-value B2B buyer segment.
- **Recommendation:** Add a TCO comparison table: cheap charger vs. certified GaN charger over 3 years for a 100-vehicle fleet.

#### Gap 2: Landed Cost Calculation for French Importers (High Priority)
French importers need to understand total cost: FOB + freight + customs + TVA + DEEE + certification.
- **Recommendation:** Add a worked example: "Combien coûte réellement l'importation de 1 000 chargeurs voiture en France ?"

#### Gap 3: Comparison with Competitor Solutions (Medium Priority)
No mention of how WOWOHCOOL compares to alternatives (Alibaba generic, other Shenzhen factories).
- **Recommendation:** Add a comparison table: WOWOHCOOL vs. Alibaba generics vs. European distributors.

#### Gap 4: French EV Tax Incentives Context (Medium Priority)
France has specific EV incentives (bonus écologique, prime à la conversion) that drive fleet electrification → more USB-C PD demand.
- **Recommendation:** Add a brief section on how French EV adoption creates aftermarket charging accessory demand.

#### Gap 5: Video/Visual Content Opportunity (Low Priority)
The article has good images but no video or interactive elements.
- **Recommendation:** Consider embedding a short factory tour video or interactive product configurator.

#### Gap 6: H1 B2B Signal Strength (Low Priority)
Current H1: "Chargeur Voiture OEM : Guide GaN V, PD 3.1 pour Importateurs" — 61 characters, contains "OEM" + "Importateurs" ✅
- Minor: Could test "Guide OEM Chargeur Voiture 2026: GaN V, PD 3.1, E-Mark pour Importateurs FR" (64 chars, adds year + E-Mark + FR geo signal)
