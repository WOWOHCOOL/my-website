## 2. Spain/LATAM Local Market Research

### 🔴 Critical: EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542 — Active Since 2024, Tightening 2026
- **Battery Passport**: Digital traceability mandatory for batteries >2kWh (starts 2026, full 2027)
- **Carbon Footprint**: Declaration mandatory from February 2026 for batteries >2kWh, third-party verified
- **Efficiency Label**: A-E rating mandatory from July 2026
- **Sanctions**: Up to 50,000€ for non-compliance
- **EPR/RAEE**: Registration + Authorized Representative in EU required
- **Spain-specific**: RD 442/2024 context (already covered in USB-C PD article)

### 🔴 Critical: UN38.3 2026 Updates
- **SOC ≤30% now applies to UN3481** (batteries packed WITH equipment) — previously only UN3480 (batteries alone)
- **New 3-meter stacking test** — old reports invalidated
- **Effective January 1, 2026** — already in force
- Air freight: cargo planes only (Class 9 dangerous goods), not passenger planes

### Semi-Solid-Specific Advantage
- Semi-solid cells pass nail penetration/crush tests without fire — easier UN38.3 certification
- Lower thermal runaway risk → smoother air freight approval
- This is a competitive moat for semi-solid over Li-Po that no Spanish article covers
