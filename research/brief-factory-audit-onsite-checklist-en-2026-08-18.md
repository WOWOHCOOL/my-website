```
H1: On-Site Factory Audit Checklist: 25 Points OEM Buyers Verify in China (60 chars)

Intro Hook
- "You booked the flight to Shenzhen. Now what do you look for?"
- The 3 audits confused: supplier verification (paperwork) vs. factory audit (physical) vs. pre-shipment inspection (goods)

H2: 1. Before the Visit: 4 Documents to Request Before Boarding
  H3: Business license (Unified Social Credit Code + registered capital)
  H3: ISO 9001 certificate — verify at the registrar
  H3: Factory address on Baidu Maps (satellite view for size sanity check)
  H3: Names of the QC manager + production manager (to greet by name on-site)

H2: 2. Zone 1 — The Gate and Reception (First 15 Minutes)
  H3: Signage in Chinese + English + factory logo
  H3: Guard log — see if other visitors are logged today
  H3: Reception area — trophies, client photo wall, samples display
  H3: Red flag: no visitors log, warehouse-only building, shared entrance

H2: 3. Zone 2 — The SMT Line (30 Minutes)
  H3: SMT machine brand and vintage (Panasonic/Yamaha/JUKI — not "brand-less")
  H3: Reflow oven — dual-side heating, temperature-profile printout
  H3: Solder paste inspection (SPI) machine present?
  H3: AOI (Automated Optical Inspection) after reflow
  H3: Red flag: manual placement, no SPI, dust on equipment

H2: 4. Zone 3 — The Assembly Line (30 Minutes)
  H3: Workers wearing anti-static wristbands + smocks
  H3: Bin labeling — parts have batch codes, not blank bags
  H3: In-process QC (IPQC) inspector on the line, not in an office
  H3: Assembly speed vs stated capacity — sanity check
  H3: Red flag: no ESD protection, mixed parts in one bin

H2: 5. Zone 4 — The Aging Room (Critical for Chargers)
  H3: 100% 4-hour aging (industry-average 2-hour batch sampling)
  H3: Aging jig — how many chargers on load at once
  H3: Temperature/current logging — request to see the day's log
  H3: Red flag: no aging room, unpowered chargers stacked, empty jigs mid-day

H2: 6. Zone 5 — The QC Lab (Live Test Demonstration)
  H3: Ask for 3 live tests, run in front of you
  H3: Test 1: Hi-Pot (dielectric withstand, 3,000 V AC, 1 s)
  H3: Test 2: Thermal (65W load for 30 min, FLIR thermal image)
  H3: Test 3: USB-C PD protocol analyzer (verify PDO negotiation)
  H3: Ripple + noise on oscilloscope (< 25 mVpp @ 15W)
  H3: Red flag: refusal to run live tests, no test equipment on the shelf

H2: 7. Zone 6 — The Warehouse and Shipping
  H3: Finished goods with bar-coded cartons + client PO numbers
  H3: Sample retention shelf (2-year retention is the ISO 9001 rule)
  H3: Shipping documentation on a wall board
  H3: Red flag: goods without labels, empty warehouse (staging factory)

H2: 8. 8 Photos You Must Take (Even if You Do Nothing Else)
  H3: The factory sign at the gate (geo-tag ON)
  H3: The SMT line brand plate on the machine
  H3: The aging room clock + running load
  H3: The QC lab test equipment (oscilloscope + thermal camera)
  H3: Certificate wall (ISO, CE, FCC) — check dates
  H3: Sample retention shelf
  H3: Business card of QC manager (verify title + name later)
  H3: Group photo with production manager (they will avoid it if they are a broker)

H2: 9. Charger-Specific Red Flags — What OEM Buyers Miss
  H3: USB-C port solder joints under 20× loupe (cold joints = field failures)
  H3: PCB conformal coating (humidity protection)
  H3: Transformer winding — Y-capacitor position (safety-critical)
  H3: MOSFET brand markings — genuine vs. remarked
  H3: Cell top labels on power banks — LG/Samsung/Panasonic vs. blank

H2: 10. Cost & When to Use Each
  H3: Supplier verification: $100-200 — pre-visit paperwork
  H3: Factory audit: $300-800 — first visit or new SKU
  H3: Pre-shipment inspection (PSI): $200-350 — every order
  H3: Third-party auditors — SGS, Bureau Veritas, QIMA, Intertek

H2: 11. FAQ (7 questions — see §5)

Conclusion + CTA
- "Book a WOWOHCOOL factory visit — 30-min walkthrough with the QC manager"
```
