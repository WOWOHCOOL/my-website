## 3. Market Data

### PD 3.1 Laptop Power Bank Market
- **TAM 2026**: $11.2B – $18.6B
- **2031 Projected**: $29.8B
- **CAGR**: ~11.4%
- **140W is the B2B sweet spot** — MacBook Pro 16″ under load, 25,000mAh (92.5Wh) aviation compliant

### PD 3.1 vs PD 3.0 — The Core Upgrade
| Feature | PD 3.0 | PD 3.1 (2026 Baseline) |
|---------|--------|----------------------|
| Max Output | 100W | 140W / 180W / **240W** |
| Voltage | 5V-20V | +28V / 36V / 48V |
| Target | Phones, Ultrabooks | MacBook Pro 16″, Gaming Laptops |

### EU Catalysts
- **USB-C mandatory for laptops since April 2026** — proprietary chargers obsolete, PD 3.1 demand accelerating
- **100Wh aviation limit** — 27,000mAh optimal design window
- **Enterprise procurement** for hybrid/remote work driving corporate volume orders

### Technology Shifts
- **GaN 5th Gen**: 22% less heat, 30% smaller
- **Solid-state breakthrough**: Hyper showcased at CES 2026; cycle life 800-1,000+
- **AI-optimized charging**: Smart multi-port power allocation
- **Thermal management IP**: Top OEMs achieve 92-96% efficiency vs 80-85% generic
