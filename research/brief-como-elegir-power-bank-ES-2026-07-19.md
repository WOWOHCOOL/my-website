## 1. Current Article Assessment

### Strengths (Keep)
- **Exceptional technical depth**: mAh conversion formulas, charge count tables, airline regulation specifics
- **Real factory data**: FOB pricing from factory-data-panel.md, MOQ tiers, certification costs
- **8 FAQ questions in Schema**: comprehensive, B2B-angled answers
- **Strong visual content**: factory QC images, capacity tier comparisons
- **11 external authority links**: FAA, IATA, USB-IF, WPC, EUR-Lex, IndexBox
- **Nina Nico expert quote**: specific, experience-based, regionally targeted (Spain, Mexico, Colombia)
- **Section 8 (Import)**: well-structured with certification tables, logistics, DDP cost breakdown

### Critical Issue: Title/Body Mismatch

**H1 says**: "Power Bank para **Importadores**: Especificaciones Clave y Selección OEM 2026"
**Body delivers**: ~70% consumer buying guide + ~30% B2B import section bolted at the end

This is the #1 problem. The article promises B2B procurement guidance but sections 1-7 read like a consumer "how to choose" guide (capacity tiers for "viajeros diarios", "estudiantes", "oficina"). The B2B reader (procurement manager) gets to section 8 before finding content relevant to their job.

**SEO Impact**: Google sees the H1 promising B2B content, but the body delivering B2C content. This intent mismatch can cause:
- Poor rankings for B2B queries (the article won't match "power bank OEM importer" intent)
- Poor rankings for B2C queries (the H1 signals B2B, confusing consumer-intent matching)
- High bounce rate from procurement managers who don't find what the title promised

| **dateModified** | 2026-07-16 | Needs update to today |

### Recommended SEO Adjustments

**Strategic Decision**: This article has two possible optimization paths:

**Path A: Embrace B2B fully** — Rewrite consumer sections (1-7) with procurement-manager framing. Rename "viajeros diarios" → "perfil de comprador", add MOQ/profit-margin angle to each tier. **Higher risk, higher reward** — targets B2B keywords exclusively.

**Path B: Split focus intentionally** — Keep consumer-friendly body but add clear B2B callout boxes in each section. Position as "guía técnica para todos los públicos con enfoque importador". **Lower risk** — captures both B2C and B2B traffic.

**→ Recommended: Path A** (aligns with site strategy; all other ES articles are B2B)
