## 4. SERP Factual Validation

### ✅ Confirmed in SERP
- Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 effective August 2023, core requirements August 2025
- CE marking mandatory for all batteries
- EPR registration is country-by-country (no single EU-wide system)
- Authorized representative mandatory for non-EU manufacturers under GPSR
- Triple EPR streams (Batteries + WEEE + Packaging under PPWR)
- Due diligence postponed to August 2027 for €150M+ operators
- Omnibus VIII (June 2026) — expanded "producer" definition + SVHC definition
- Digital product passport / QR code mandatory from February 2027
- Fines up to 4% of annual turnover
- Stiftung EAR for Germany, Ecopilas/ERP for Spain, EcoOrganisme for France

### ⚠️ Minor discrepancies noted (article is correct per ES source + factory data panel)
- Some SERP sources use "18 August 2025" vs our "August 18, 2025" — both correct, format preference
- Lead limit: SERP variously reports 0.01% or 0.004% — article uses 0.01% consistent with ES source and Cooley analysis

### 🔍 Unique content not found in SERP (Information Gain)
1. PPWR August 12, 2026 packaging EPR deadline — **zero SERP competitors mention this**
2. Certification cost table with real USD pricing per model
3. WOWOHCOOL authorized representative included with OEM orders
4. Factory footprint data (5,000 m², ISO 9001, 50+ R&D, since 2013)
5. Bosch case study context
