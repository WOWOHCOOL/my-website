- 50% of new cars have built-in wireless → aftermarket retrofit growing

### Key Drivers
1. **Qi2 standard adoption** → 63%→75% penetration in 2026
2. **Hands-free legislation** → converting mounts from convenience to necessity
3. **Higher power (15→25W)** → heat = active cooling demand
4. **EV market expansion** → cable-free charging demand
5. **62% of drivers** report phone overheating concern during in-car charging

### TEC Cooling Premium
- Consumers willing to pay **15-20% premium** for active cooling
- TEC + fan: maintains 33-34°C surface at 25W/30min
- Without cooling: throttling after 10-15 min
