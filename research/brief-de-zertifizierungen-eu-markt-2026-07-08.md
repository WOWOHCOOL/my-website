## Key Data Points (2026)

### CE Framework
- EN 62368-1: mandatory safety standard (replaced 60950-1 + 60065)
- EMC: EN 55032 (emissions) + EN 55035 (immunity, superseded EN 55024)
- DoC must be in importer's name — factory's DoC is not sufficient
- Technical file: 10 years retention after last placement

### Certification Costs
- LVD + EMC (standard charger): $700–$1,400
- Full EU package (LVD + EMC + ErP + RoHS): $1,400–$2,800
- Multi-market (US + EU parallel): $8,000–$15,000
- RoHS 2.0 standalone: $210–$560

### Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 — Timeline
- 18.02.2024: In force
- 18.08.2025: New labeling + EPR registration per country
- 2026: CO₂ footprint report + recycled content
- 18.02.2027: User-replaceable batteries mandatory
- Blei limit: 0.01% (10× stricter than RoHS!)

### Additional Requirements
- GPSR (EU 2023/988): EU Authorized Representative mandatory
- WEEE: Registration per country (Stiftung EAR in DE)
- Battery EPR: Separate from WEEE registration (both required)
- Ökodesign (EU 2025/2052): USB-C mandate for wireless chargers, cables by Dec 2028
