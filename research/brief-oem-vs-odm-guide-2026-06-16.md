## 6. Hook 草稿

> Starting a charging accessories brand in 2026? Choosing between **OEM** (you design, factory builds) and **ODM** (factory designs, you brand) decides three things: how fast you launch, how much capital you tie up, and whether your product looks different from competitors on Amazon.
>
> **Quick answer**: ODM if budget < $20K and MOQ ≤ 500 units. OEM if budget > $50K and you have proprietary tech (GaN V, Qi2 MPP, semi-solid-state battery). Hybrid if $20K–$50K and you want differentiation without full tooling investment.
>
> This guide draws on 10+ years of running an ISO 9001 certified charger factory in Shenzhen — including OEM contracts with Bosch and Jacob Jensen, and 200+ active ODM/OEM client brands shipping globally.
