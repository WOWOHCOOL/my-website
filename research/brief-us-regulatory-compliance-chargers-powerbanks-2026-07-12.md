## 1. Quick Reference: US vs EU Certification

| Requirement | US | EU |
|------------|----|-----|
| **Safety** | UL 2056 / UL 62368-1 | CE (LVD + EMV) / EN 62368-1 |
| **EMC** | FCC Part 15 (SDoC or ID) | CE (EMV 2014/30/EU) |
| **Battery Transport** | UN38.3 + DOT PHMSA | UN38.3 + ADR |
| **Energy Efficiency** | DOE Level VI (federal) + CEC (California) | EU Ecodesign 2019/1782 |
| **Chemical Safety** | California Prop 65 | REACH + RoHS |
| **Wireless Charging** | FCC ID mandatory | RED 2014/53/EU |
| **Retail Requirement** | Amazon/Walmart require UL | CE sufficient for most EU retail |

## 2. FCC — Federal Communications Commission

### Certification Paths
| Path | Applies To | Process | Cost (2026) | Timeline |
|------|-----------|---------|:-----------:|:--------:|
| **FCC SDoC** | Wired chargers, non-wireless power banks | Self-test + declaration | $400-800 | 2-4 weeks |
| **FCC ID** | Qi/wireless chargers, Bluetooth-enabled devices | FCC-accredited lab + TCB review | $1,200-5,000 | 4-8 weeks |

**Key Rule:** Any product with wireless charging (Qi, Qi2) = FCC ID mandatory. Wired-only chargers = SDoC suffices.

### Verification
- Check existing FCC IDs at **fccid.io**
- Verify: Grantee name matches supplier, product description matches, date is valid
- **Never accept a photocopy** — FCC ID must be verifiable in the public database

### Penalties
- Customs seizure / denied entry
- Amazon delisting within 48 hours of complaint
- CPB can require re-export or destruction

## 3. UL — Underwriters Laboratories

### Applicable Standards
| Standard | Product | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| **UL 2056** | Power banks | US-specific power bank safety standard |
| **UL 62368-1** | Chargers, power adapters | Harmonized with IEC 62368-1 |
| **UL 1642** | Lithium cells | Individual cell testing |
| **UL 62133 / IEC 62133-2** | Portable lithium batteries/packs | 2026 mainstream standard |

### Why UL is "Mandatory in Practice"
- Amazon, Walmart, Best Buy, Target all require UL listing
- 40,000+ US local jurisdictions reference UL standards
- Without UL → no major retail channel access
- UL requires **annual factory follow-up inspections** (ongoing cost)

### Cost & Timeline
- Initial testing: **$1,500-15,000** (varies by complexity)
- Timeline: **6-12 weeks**
- Annual factory audit: ~$2,000-4,000/year

## 4. California Proposition 65

### What Triggers Prop 65 for Chargers/Power Banks
- **Lead** — solder, PVC insulation, components
- **Phthalates** (DEHP, DINP) — PVC cable jackets, strain reliefs, plastic housings
- **Cadmium** — contacts/plating
- **BPA** — some plastic components

### Importer Obligations
- Affix **warning label** on product, packaging, or point-of-sale
- Warning must include official Prop 65 symbol + chemical name
- Liability falls on the **importer/distributor**, not the factory
- Fines: **$2,500 per violation per day**
- Products sold **only outside California** are exempt — but Amazon ships nationwide

### Practical Steps
- Request Prop 65 compliance data from factory
- If unavailable: commission material testing (lead, phthalates, cadmium, BPA)
- Add compliant warning labels to all US-bound products as default (safest approach)

## 5. DOE Level VI — Energy Efficiency

### Federal Mandate (10 CFR Part 430)
All external power supplies and battery chargers sold in the US must meet Level VI:

| Spec | ≤ 49W | > 49W ≤ 250W |
|------|-------|-------------|
| No-Load Power | ≤ 0.100 W | ≤ 0.210 W |
| Average Active Efficiency | ≥ Level VI tier | ≥ 87-92% |

### Importer Obligations
- **Lab test** to DOE Appendix Z (ISO 17025 accredited lab)
- **File certification** in DOE CCMS database **BEFORE** first US sale
- Mark product with **Roman numeral VI** on nameplate
- Retain test records **5 years minimum**
- Importer is legally the "manufacturer" — factory filing doesn't exempt you

### Penalties
- Civil penalties per noncompliant unit — one container = massive liability
- Customs may flag uncertified products

## 6. California CEC (Additional Layer)

### California Energy Commission — Title 20
- Products must meet CEC efficiency limits (often tighter than DOE)
- Test by **CEC-approved lab** (NVLAP/A2LA accredited)
- Register in **CEC MAEDbS database** — unique registration number issued
- Registration number must appear on product label/packaging

### 2026 Trends
- No-load power targets dropping toward ≤ 0.04 W for low-power chargers
- Zero-standby power discussions underway
- USB-C PD multi-voltage chargers: test at "most typical" voltage setting

---

## 7. UN38.3 — Battery Transport (Universal)

### Mandatory Tests (8 items)
Altitude simulation, thermal test, vibration, shock, external short circuit, overcharge, forced discharge, impact/crush

### Additional Requirements
- MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheet) required
- DOT PHMSA: >100Wh power banks banned from air transport
- Airlines require clear certification markings
