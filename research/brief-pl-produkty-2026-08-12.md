### Key Insight: WOWOHCOOL is the ONLY real manufacturer appearing in Polish B2B search results
- Baltrade and Itsell are **resellers/distributors** — they buy from Chinese factories and resell
- Citron does **promotional power banks** — not the same category
- **No Polish-language competitor positions themselves as a factory-direct OEM manufacturer**
- WOWOHCOOL appeared on page 1 for "power bank z Chin producent" — **already ranking**

### Common Themes from Polish Competitor Pages
- Heavy emphasis on **certification compliance** (CE, RoHS) — Polish market is regulation-conscious
- Price-focused positioning ("ceny hurtowe", "rabaty dla stałych klientów")
- Fast domestic shipping as a selling point (same-day from Polish warehouse)
- Product specifications listed but no manufacturing insight

### Content Gaps (What Polish Competitors Don't Cover)
1. ❌ No factory tour / manufacturing process transparency
2. ❌ No MOQ flexibility explanation (OEM vs ODM vs stock)
3. ❌ No customs clearance guidance for Polish importers (ISZTAR, PUESC)
4. ❌ No real technical specs (GaN V vs GaN IV, N52H magnets, PD 3.1 EPR)
5. ❌ No lead time transparency (25-30 days OEM, 45-60 days ODM)
6. ❌ No B2B case studies or client references in Polish
7. ❌ No information about shipping routes to Poland (Gdańsk/Gdynia sea freight)

### Differentiation Strategy
WOWOHCOOL's unique advantages over Polish competitors:
1. **Real factory, not reseller** — 5,000㎡, ISO 9001, 200+ global brands
2. **Direct pricing** — no middleman margin, FOB Shenzhen
3. **Full OEM/ODM capability** — ID design, PCBA, tooling, certification support
4. **Technology leadership** — semi-solid-state, GaN V, Qi2 MPP, PD 3.1 — none of which Polish distributors mention
5. **Poland-specific logistics** — sea freight to Gdańsk/Gdynia, customs documentation

## 3. Current Page Audit (vs. B2B Quality Gates)

### What's Working ✅
- H1 contains B2B signal words (Produkty OEM/ODM)
- Product grid is comprehensive (6 power bank subcategories + wireless + GaN + car chargers)
- FAQ section with 5 B2B-relevant questions (Poland-specific answers)

- Poland-specific details: Gdańsk/Gdynia port, ISZTAR, PUESC, UOKiK, Polish CE declaration

### What Needs Improvement ⚠️

| Issue | Severity | Details |
|-------|:--------:|---------|
| **H1 too generic** | High | "Nasze Produkty" is a branding headline, not an SEO H1. Should contain primary keyword |
| **Missing H2 B2B signals** | High | H2s are product names only ("Power Bank", "Ładowarka Bezprzewodowa") — need B2B modifiers like "producent OEM", "fabryka Shenzhen" |
| **No quick answer / value prop above the fold** | Medium | Hero paragraph is descriptive but doesn't immediately answer "Why WOWOHCOOL vs Polish distributor?" |
| **Product descriptions too short** | Medium | Each product card has only 6-8 words. Could add spec highlights (W, mAh, GaN generation, Qi2 version) |
| **No author/EEAT signals on page** | Medium | FAQ has no author attribution — should reference company expert |
| **Search functionality exists but no schema markup for it** | Low | Sitelinks Search Box schema missing |
| **Missing Organization schema details** | Low | ManufacturingBusiness schema exists but lacks foundingDate, employee count, area |
| **Page word count** | Low | ~550 words (Polish text only, excluding HTML) — below the 800-1200 target for product pages |
| **No inline links to service pages** | Medium | CTA at bottom links to /pl/uslugi-oem-odm/ but no contextual links within product descriptions |
| **SchemaItemList URLs inconsistent** | High | Uses English paths (`/pl/produkty/power-bank`) instead of Polish paths (`/pl/produkty/power-bank/`) — trailing slash mismatch may exist |
