## 4. Market Context

### EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542 Timeline
| Date | Requirement | Impact on Power Banks |
|------|------------|----------------------|
| 18 Aug 2024 | CE marking mandatory | Already handled by WOWOHCOOL |
| 18 Aug 2025 | EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility) | WOWOHCOOL offers EAR registration |
| **18 Aug 2026** | **Enhanced labelling (QR code, 10 data points)** | Coming in 1 month — urgent messaging opportunity |
| **18 Feb 2027** | **Removability & replaceability (Article 11)** | Major design challenge for glued enclosures |
| 18 Aug 2027 | Supply chain due diligence | Applies to €40M+ turnover companies |

**Key insight**: The Aug 2026 labelling deadline is 1 month away. This is a strong hook for content updates.

### Semi-Solid Market Phase (2026)
According to OEM industry analysis, 2026 is the **"yield stabilization phase"** for semi-solid power banks:
- Manufacturing yields are improving
- Costs are declining but still 20-40% premium over Li-Polymer
- Early adopters are premium brands and safety-focused markets (EU)
- Mass replacement of Li-ion not expected until 2028+
