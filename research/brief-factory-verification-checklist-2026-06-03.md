## 4. Optimization Recommendations

### Priority 1: Content Enhancements (High Impact)

#### A. Add "Trading Company vs. Real Factory" Detection Section
- Registered capital thresholds (¥1-10M = manufacturer vs ¥100-500K = trader)
- Multiple unrelated product categories = red flag
- Pristine showroom + minimal production equipment warning
- Electric bill check (<¥3,000/month = not manufacturing)
- Employee count vs. claimed capacity ratio

#### B. Add Government Database Verification Steps
- National Enterprise Credit Information System (gsxt.gov.cn) for Unified Social Credit Code
- China Judgments Online (wenshu.court.gov.cn) for legal disputes
- Customs Registration Certificate verification
- How to read and interpret a Chinese business license

#### C. Add Remote/Video Audit Best Practices
- Live video walkthrough checklist (what to ask to show)
- Same-day proof tactics (newspaper with date, whiteboard with buyer name)
- Satellite/street view cross-reference (Google Maps + Baidu Maps)
- Video call vs. on-site: when each is appropriate
- Red flags during video tours (pre-recorded footage, restricted areas)

#### D. Strengthen Sample Evaluation for Charging Products
- PD protocol negotiation testing (5V/9V/12V/20V)
- Real Wh output vs. rated mAh (conversion efficiency measurement)
- Thermal profile under fast-charge load (IR thermometer method)
