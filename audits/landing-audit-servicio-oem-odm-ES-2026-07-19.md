## Prioritized Action Items

### High Priority
1. **Add mid-page secondary CTA** — After the process section (step 6), the single "Solicitar plazo personalizado" button could benefit from a secondary "Solicitar muestras gratuitas" option. Lower-friction entry point for top-of-funnel visitors. Expected CVR lift: +10-15%
2. **Add "Equipo hispanohablante" visual badge** — Spanish support is mentioned in FAQ but could be more prominent in the hero or after the process section as a trust element

### Medium Priority
3. **A/B test hero CTA copy** — "Solicitar presupuesto" vs "Recibir catálogo y precios" — catalog request is lower commitment
4. **Add a factory video or virtual tour CTA** — Between the cost guide and case studies, a "Ver nuestra fábrica en 60 segundos" video link would increase trust for remote buyers
5. **Add an urgency element** — "Plazos de producción para Q3 2026: reserve su slot" — gentle manufacturing timeline urgency

### Low Priority
6. **Mobile optimization** — Test the OEM vs ODM comparison cards on mobile (side-by-side desktop layout may stack poorly)
7. **Add trust bar numbers** between hero and OEM/ODM sections — quick stats (200+ brands, 1M+/month, 50+ engineers)

---

## A/B Test Suggestions

1. **Hero CTA**: "Solicitar presupuesto" vs "Recibir catálogo y precios" — test commitment level
2. **WhatsApp placement**: Hero vs sticky bottom bar — test mobile engagement
