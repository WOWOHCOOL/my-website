## Prioritized Action Items

### High Priority
1. **Add mid-page CTA** — Insert "Solicitar visita virtual" or "Ver catálogo de productos" between the WOW/OH/COOL and Factory sections. Currently there's a long scroll without a click target. Expected CVR lift: +15-20%
2. **Add named client testimonial section** — Pull Jacob Jensen quote from case studies page. "WOWOHCOOL solved our complex ODM challenge — 6,000 units, zero defects" with attribution is more powerful than inline mentions. Expected trust lift: +10%

### Medium Priority
3. **A/B test "Solicitar catálogo" vs "Solicitar presupuesto"** — "Catálogo" is lower friction and may convert more top-of-funnel visitors
4. **Add a factory photo gallery or video embed** — Currently has 3 good images. A short factory tour video (30-60s) would dramatically increase trust for remote buyers
5. **Add "Equipo hispanohablante" highlight** — Spanish-language support is mentioned once. Make it more prominent — it's a key differentiator for Spanish-speaking importers

### Low Priority
6. **Mobile CTA sticky bar** — On mobile, the hero CTA disappears on scroll. A sticky "Solicitar presupuesto" bar would improve mobile CVR
7. **Add page load counter / live social proof** — "X importers requested quotes this month" — dynamic social proof
8. **Consider adding a "Certifications" visual strip** — ISO 9001, CE, Qi2 logos as a visual row rather than text mentions

## A/B Test Suggestions

1. **Hero headline A/B**: "Fabricante OEM/ODM en Shenzhen" vs "Su Fábrica de Cargadores en China — Sin Intermediarios" — test authority vs benefit framing
