### 1. "6. El modelo híbrido: de ODM a OEM en 3 fases" — Score: 60/100

**Problem:** Narrative opening, not a definition or answer. The answer (what the hybrid model IS) is delayed until the 3rd sentence. AI would struggle to extract a clean definition.
