### 5.1 Dangling Comma in Expert Insight (Line 488)
```html
<p class="text-sm text-slate-500 mt-2">, Snowy May, Market Manager en WOWOHCOOL</p>
```
The attribution line starts with a comma-space. Fix: remove `, ` prefix.

### 5.2 Missing Table Cell Data (Line 564)
```html
<td class="text-center px-5 py-4 text-sm text-slate-600">, </td>
```
The "Cuota" column for "Power bank alta potencia (65W+)" contains only a comma. This appears to be missing data. Should contain a market share percentage.

### 5.3 No H3 Sub-headings -- Flat Structure
All 9 content H2 sections are single-level blocks. No H3 sub-divisions exist within sections. This:
- Reduces Featured Snippet extraction opportunities (no H3 → direct answer structure)
- Limits AI content navigation (crawlers use heading hierarchy for semantic mapping)
- Makes longer sections (Section 2 GaN V, Section 5 EU Directive) harder to scan

**Recommendation**: Add 1-3 H3 sub-headings to sections longer than 3 paragraphs, using data-conclusion format (ej: "¿Por que el sobrecoste GaN cayo del 45% al 22% entre 2024 y 2026?").

### 5.4 FAQ Count at Minimum
5 FAQ questions is the absolute minimum (standard requires 5-8). Consider adding 2-3 more:
- "¿Que certificaciones necesita un cargador OEM para vender en Espana?" (CE + AENOR + UNE-EN)
- "¿Cual es el MOQ tipico para cargadores GaN V OEM desde fabrica en Shenzhen?"
