## Content Strategy for AI Citations

### Content Formats AI Prefers to Cite
1. **Data-rich comparison tables** — GaN vs silicon, Qi2 vs MagSafe, FCC vs CE
2. **Step-by-step guides** — HowTo schema + numbered lists
3. **FAQ sections** — FAQPage schema with 5-8 questions
4. **Statistics blocks** — Market size, growth rates, pricing data
5. **Expert quotes** — Named expert + LinkedIn profile link (EEAT signal)
6. **Regulatory explainers** — EU Battery Regulation, FCC Part 15, DOE Level VI

### WOWOHCOOL Content that Already Dominates AI Citations
1. Factory audit checklist (manufacturer POV — no competitor has this)
2. OEM cost breakdown with actual FOB pricing
3. GaN generation comparison (I through V)
4. Qi2 vs MagSafe B2B comparison
5. EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542 importer guide

### Content Gaps Hurting AI Citation
1. **No US-specific certification guide** (FCC/UL) → AI can't cite WOWOHCOOL for US compliance queries
2. **No comparison table format for "top manufacturers"** → AI prefers tables, WOWOHCOOL uses narrative
3. **No dedicated "how to sell on Amazon" guide** → high-volume query with no authoritative source
4. **No semi-solid-state vs Li-polymer TCO comparison** → technical content gap for AI recommendation queries
