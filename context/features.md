## Core Value Propositions

### 1. **Semi-Solid-State Power Banks**
- **Feature**: Semi-solid-state battery technology with built-in retractable or fixed cables (USB-C + Lightning). Capacities from 5,000mAh to 10,000mAh. Supports PD 3.1 up to 65W and Qi2.2 wireless charging.
- **Benefit**: 50% thinner profile than traditional Li-Po power banks. 30% higher energy density. Significantly reduced fire risk — safer for air travel and daily carry. Distinctive technology that differentiates your brand on Amazon and retail shelves.
- **Conversion Angle**: "The only semi-solid-state power bank featured at CES 2026 — 50% thinner, 30% more energy density, and air-travel safe. Available for OEM/ODM with your branding."

### 2. **Qi2 Wireless Chargers**
- **Feature**: Qi2 MPP (Magnetic Power Profile) certified wireless chargers with N52H neodymium magnets. 15W universal fast charging for iPhone and Android. Multiple form factors: 3-in-1 folding stations, wine glass 360° rotatable, car mounts, zinc alloy premium docks.
- **Benefit**: Universal 15W fast charging matching Apple MagSafe performance without Apple certification costs. Secure magnetic alignment works even with protective cases. Premium build quality (zinc alloy, aluminum) elevates brand perception.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Qi2 certified with N52H magnets — 15W universal fast charging for iPhone and Android. Choose from 10+ designs: foldable, car mount, night light, or ice-cooling. OEM/ODM with custom CMF."

### 3. **GaN V Chargers**
- **Feature**: 5th generation Gallium Nitride (GaN V) technology. Power range from 20W to 240W PD 3.1. Multi-port configurations including retractable cable + wireless charging combo models. 10-layer circuit protection.
- **Benefit**: 40% smaller than traditional silicon chargers. 30% better heat dissipation. Supports PD 3.1, PPS, QC 5.0, SCP, AFC for universal device compatibility. Eliminates the need for multiple chargers.
- **Conversion Angle**: "GaN V chargers 40% smaller than silicon, PD 3.1 up to 240W. From ultra-compact 35W travel chargers to 100W multi-port desktop stations. Our retractable cable GaN charger with built-in wireless pad is an Amazon best-seller."

### 4. **GaN Car Chargers**
- **Feature**: GaN V car chargers with PD 3.1 up to 140W. Built-in 0.8m auto-retractable USB-C cable. 12V-24V wide input range for all vehicles. Digital voltage display. Smart IC chip identification for broad device compatibility.
- **Benefit**: Fast-charge laptops, tablets, and phones simultaneously in the car. Retractable cable eliminates clutter. 24V compatibility works for trucks and commercial fleets. GaN technology keeps the charger cool during extended use.
- **Conversion Angle**: "105W-140W GaN car charger with auto-retractable cable — fast-charge a MacBook Pro and iPhone simultaneously. Built for 12V-24V vehicles. PD 3.1, E-Mark certified. Your brand, our manufacturing."

### 5. **Heating Apparel Batteries**
- **Feature**: Specialized heating batteries for heated clothing. Output voltages: 7.4V DC and 12.6V DC. Capacities from 2,000mAh to 10,000mAh. Custom wire length, connector types, and voltage configurations.
- **Benefit**: Reliable power source for heated jackets, vests, socks, gloves, scarves, massage pillows, and hand warmers. CE, FCC, RoHS, PSE certified. Customizable for any heating apparel brand.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Specialized heating batteries for apparel brands. 7.4V-12.6V, CE/FCC/RoHS/PSE certified. Custom connectors, wire lengths, and packaging — MOQ 500 units."

### 6. **2-in-1 Hybrid Chargers (Power Bank + Wall Charger)**
- **Feature**: Dual-function device combining a GaN wall adapter (up to 67W) with a built-in 10,000mAh power bank. Integrated foldable AC plug and retractable USB-C cable.
- **Benefit**: One device replaces both a wall charger and a power bank. Never forget to charge your power bank — it charges itself when plugged into the wall. Ideal for travelers, remote workers, and commuters.
- **Conversion Angle**: "45W-67W 2-in-1 hybrid: wall charger + 10,000mAh power bank in one device. Perfect for travel and EDC. OEM/ODM ready with custom branding and packaging."

### 7. **High-Power PD 3.1 Power Stations**
- **Feature**: High-capacity power banks from 67W to 240W PD 3.1. Capacities up to 27,000mAh (99.9Wh, airline-safe). TFT smart display showing real-time power stats. Multiple ports including USB-C, USB-A, and DC.
- **Benefit**: Powers laptops (MacBook Pro, Dell XPS, ThinkPad), tablets, drones, and cameras. The TFT display provides professional-grade power monitoring. 99.9Wh design maximizes capacity while staying within airline travel limits.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Up to 240W PD 3.1 power station with TFT display — charge any laptop at full speed. 27,000mAh capacity, airline-safe. OEM/ODM with custom CMF and port configuration."

### 8. **Power Strips with USB-C PD**
- **Feature**: Desktop power strips combining AC outlets (2-3 sockets) with USB-C PD ports (20W-65W) and USB-A ports. GaN technology for compact size. Available with or without surge protection.
- **Benefit**: One desktop device powers laptops, phones, monitors, and desk accessories. USB-C PD built-in eliminates the need for separate phone chargers. Compact GaN design saves desk space.
- **Conversion Angle**: "Desktop power strip with built-in 65W USB-C PD — power your entire workspace from one outlet. GaN compact design. OEM/ODM available for office supply and tech accessory brands."

## Common Questions & Objections

### "What is your MOQ?"
**Answer**: Our MOQ starts at 500 units per SKU for standard OEM branding (logo + packaging). For deep ODM customization with new tooling, MOQ is 2,000 units. We offer flexible options for startup brands — contact us to discuss your requirements.

### "How do I know your quality is consistent?"
**Answer**: We implement a 4-stage QC process: IQC (incoming component inspection), IPQC (in-process monitoring), FQC (final functional test), and OQC (outgoing quality check). 100% of products undergo a 4-hour aging test before shipping. We also welcome third-party inspections.

### "What certifications do I need to sell in my market?"
**Answer**: We handle certification documentation for CE (Europe), FCC (US), RoHS (global), Qi2 (wireless), UN38.3 (battery transport), PSE (Japan), KC (Korea), and UL (US). Our team guides you through the entire certification process.

### "Can you customize the design, not just the logo?"
**Answer**: Yes. Our OEM/ODM service includes full CMF (Color/Material/Finish) customization, custom PCBA with specific charging protocols, ID design (industrial design), custom tooling, and custom packaging. 50+ R&D engineers on staff.

### "What are your payment terms?"
**Answer**: Standard terms are T/T: 30% deposit to start production, 70% balance before shipment. We also offer L/C for qualified clients. Payment via bank transfer, PayPal, or Alibaba Trade Assurance.

### "How long does production take?"

### "Can you ship to my Amazon FBA warehouse?"
**Answer**: Yes. We offer warehousing and fulfillment services for Amazon FBA and other e-commerce platforms. We can ship directly to your customers, fulfillment centers, or your own warehouse. FOB, EXW, and DDP options available.

### "What warranty do you offer?"
