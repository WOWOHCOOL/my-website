# WOWOHCOOL Style Guide

This guide defines writing conventions, formatting standards, and editorial guidelines for all WOWOHCOOL content — website, product pages, blog posts, sales materials, and marketing communications.

---

## Grammar & Mechanics

### Capitalization

**Headlines & Subheadings**:
- Use **Sentence case** (capitalize first word and proper nouns only)
- Exception: Product names and SKUs (WOP67, WOW93, WOC42) always capitalized

**Brand & Product Names**:
- **WOWOHCOOL**: Always uppercase. Never "Wowohcool" or "WowOhCool"
- **Dong Yi Technology Co., Ltd**: Full legal name on first mention, then "Dong Yi Technology"
- **Product SKUs**: Always uppercase with series prefix: WOP (power bank), WOW (wireless), WOC (car charger)
- **Qi2**: Capital Q, lowercase i, number 2. Never "QI2" or "Qi 2"
- **GaN**: Capital G, lowercase a, capital N. Never "GAN" or "gan"
- **PD 3.1**: PD uppercase, space, version number. Similarly: QC 5.0, USB-C
- **MagSafe**: Capital M, lowercase a, capital S, lowercase afe

**Industry Terms**:
- OEM/ODM: Always uppercase
- CE, FCC, RoHS, PSE, KC, UL: Always uppercase (certifications)
- UN38.3: Always uppercase
- ISO 9001: Always uppercase
- FOB, EXW, DDP: Always uppercase (shipping terms)

### Numbers

**When to Spell Out**:
- Spell out: One through nine ("five units," "three samples")
- Use numerals: 10 and above ("50+ engineers," "200+ brands," "1M+ monthly")
- Exceptions:
  - Percentages: Always use numerals ("40% smaller," "30% better")
  - Money: Always use numerals ("$6.00-$15.00 per unit")
  - Measurements: Always use numerals ("5,000㎡," "0.8m cable")
  - Specs: Always use numerals ("15W," "65W," "240W")
  - Years: Always use numerals ("Since 2013")

**Large Numbers**:
- 1,000+ (use comma separator)
- 1 million (spell out "million," "billion")
- "1M+" acceptable in headlines and marketing copy

### Punctuation

**Oxford Comma**: Yes, use the Oxford comma in all lists.
- ✅ "iPhone, Apple Watch, and AirPods"
- ❌ "iPhone, Apple Watch and AirPods"

**Em Dashes**: Use em dash (—) with no spaces for emphasis or parenthetical statements.
- ✅ "GaN V technology — 40% smaller than silicon — keeps these chargers cool."

**Quotation Marks**: Use straight quotes in markdown source.

**Ellipses**: Use sparingly. Three dots, no spaces before or after. Only for omitted text or deliberate pauses.

### Abbreviations & Acronyms

**First Use**: Spell out on first use with acronym in parentheses, then use acronym thereafter.
- ✅ "Gallium Nitride (GaN) technology... GaN V delivers..."
- ✅ "Power Delivery 3.1 (PD 3.1) protocol... PD 3.1 supports..."

**Common Acronyms** (no need to spell out):
- USB, USB-C, LED, LCD, PC, iOS, Android, iPhone, MacBook

**Always Spell Out** (no acronym on first use):
- QC = Quick Charge (spell out at least once per article)
- MPP = Magnetic Power Profile
- CMF = Color/Material/Finish

## Word Choice & Usage

### Preferred Terms

**Say This** → **Not That**:
- OEM/ODM manufacturer → Factory / supplier (too generic)
- Manufacturing facility → Factory (preferred for professionalism)
- Fast charging → Super charging, quick charge (unless referring to QC protocol)
- GaN V / Gallium Nitride → GaN (specify generation when relevant)
- Semi-solid-state battery → Solid-state battery (different technology — be precise)
- Qi2 MPP → Qi2 wireless (clarify when relevant)
- Inquire / request → Contact us (too formal)
- Sample → Free sample / demo unit (when applicable)
- Factory-direct pricing → Wholesale price (factory-direct emphasizes advantage)
- Customization → Custom options (when listing capabilities)

**Product Series Naming**:
- WOP = Power Bank Series
- WOW = Wireless Charger Series
- WOC = Car Charger Series
- Always pair SKU with descriptive name on first mention: "WOP67 45W 2-in-1 Hybrid Charger"

### Words to Avoid
- "Cutting-edge" (prefer specific technology names: GaN V, Qi2 MPP)
- "Game-changing" (overused, vague — show the spec instead)
- "State-of-the-art" (meaningless — specify what makes it advanced)
- "Revolutionary" (use "next-generation" or specific innovation claims)
- "World-class" (use "ISO 9001 certified" or specific capabilities)
- "Best-in-class" (use comparison data: "40% smaller," "30% higher density")
- "Very," "really," "actually" (usually unnecessary filler)
- "Click here" (use descriptive anchor text instead)

### Inclusive Language
- Use gender-neutral language ("they" instead of "he/she")
- Avoid culturally specific idioms that don't translate globally
- Recognize global audience (Asia, Europe, Americas, Middle East)
- Lead with factual capability, not cultural stereotypes about "Chinese manufacturing"

## Formatting Standards

### Text Formatting

**Bold**:
- Use for: Key specs, product SKUs, important takeaways
- Don't overuse: Reserve for critical information buyers scan for
- ✅ "**MOQ**: 500 units for OEM orders. **Lead time**: 25-30 days."

**Italics**:
- Use for: Emphasis (sparingly), foreign phrases, product feature highlights

**ALL CAPS**:
- Avoid in body text. Use only for: standard acronyms (CE, FCC, QC, PD, GaN)

### Lists

**Bulleted Lists**:
- Use for: Features, capabilities, certifications, benefits
- Capitalize first word of each bullet
- Use period only if the item is a complete sentence
- Maintain parallel structure across all items

**Numbered Lists**:
- Use for: Sequential processes (ordering steps, QC stages)
- Format: 1. 2. 3. (period after number)
- Same capitalization and punctuation rules as bullets

**Nested Lists**: Maximum 2 levels deep. Use proper indentation.

### Links

**Anchor Text**:
- Descriptive and specific (tell the reader where they're going)
- 2-5 words typically
- ✅ "Request free samples" / "Download wholesale catalog" / "Get factory pricing"
- ❌ "Click here" / "Read more" / "Learn more" (use judiciously)

**External Links**: Open in new tab. Internal pages: same tab.

### Callout Boxes / Asides

**When to Use**:
- Client testimonials and case studies
- Key specifications or certifications highlight
- Important process notes (lead times, MOQ)
- CTA buttons or offers

**Format**: Use blockquote for testimonials. Use separated sections for specs.

## Content Structure

### Product Page Introduction
**Standard Structure** (100-200 words):
1. **Category + Credibility** (1-2 sentences): What product line + since when + certifications
2. **Product Range** (2-3 sentences): What's included in the lineup, key differentiators
3. **OEM/ODM Value** (1-2 sentences): Customization options, MOQ, target audience
4. **CTA** (1 sentence): Clear next step (request samples, catalog, quote)

### Blog Article — Introduction
**Standard Structure** (150-250 words):
1. **Hook** (1-2 sentences): Industry trend, statistic, or buyer pain point
2. **Context** (2-3 sentences): Why this matters for the reader's business
3. **Promise** (2-3 sentences): What the reader will learn / how they benefit

### Blog Article — Body
- **Section minimum**: 150 words per H2 section
- **Section maximum**: 500 words (split into H3 subsections if longer)
- **Keyword placement**: Primary keyword in first 100 words and at least one H2

### Conclusion (150-200 words)
1. **Key takeaways** (3-5 bullet recap or summary paragraph)
2. **CTA** (Request samples, contact sales, download catalog)
3. **Forward-looking** (optional, 1 sentence about partnership potential)

## SEO-Specific Style

### Meta Titles
- 50-60 characters including spaces
- Format: Primary Keyword + Secondary Modifier | WOWOHCOOL
- Include year only if time-sensitive content
- Examples:
  - "Wireless Charger OEM/ODM Manufacturer | WOWOHCOOL China"
  - "GaN Charger Factory PD 3.1 240W | WOWOHCOOL OEM Supplier"

### Meta Descriptions
- 150-160 characters including spaces
- Include primary keyword and 1-2 secondary keywords
- Include specific number or certification
- End with implied CTA
- Example: "CE/FCC/Qi2 certified wireless charger manufacturer in Shenzhen since 2013. OEM/ODM with MOQ 500+. Factory-direct pricing. Request free samples."

### URL Slugs
- Lowercase only, hyphens between words
- 3-5 words, include primary keyword
- No dates, no underscores
- Format: `/products/car-charger.html`, `/products/power-bank.html`

### Alt Text
- Accurately describe the product shown
- Include primary keyword naturally (not forced)
- 125 characters or less
- No "image of" or "picture of"
- ✅ "WOW93 3-in-1 folding Qi2 wireless charger with night light"
- ❌ "Image of product"

## Certification & Spec Reference

Always format certifications consistently:
- **CE**: European conformity
- **FCC**: US Federal Communications Commission
- **RoHS**: Restriction of Hazardous Substances
- **Qi2**: Wireless Power Consortium standard (v2.0+)
- **UN38.3**: United Nations battery transport safety
- **PD 3.1**: USB Power Delivery 3.1 (up to 240W)
- **GaN V**: 5th generation Gallium Nitride
- **QC 5.0**: Qualcomm Quick Charge 5.0
- **PPS**: Programmable Power Supply
- **E-Mark**: European automotive certification

## Voice & Tone Reminders

### Core Voice Characteristics
1. **Factory authority** — specific, proof-driven, capabilities-focused
2. **Technical precision** — accurate specs, proper terminology, benefit-linked
3. **Solution-oriented** — consultative, buyer-focused, partnership-minded
4. **Global trust** — certification-aware, internationally minded
5. **Innovation-forward** — next-gen tech, production-ready, not vaporware

### Tone Variations
- **Product pages**: Benefit-driven, spec-supported, comparison-ready
- **Blog/educational**: Authoritative, data-rich, actionable for buyers
- **OEM/ODM service**: Process-oriented, reassuring, value-demonstrating
- **Sales/CTAs**: Direct, low-friction, specific (not pushy)

- [ ] Spelling checked

- [ ] Oxford comma used consistently
- [ ] Numbers formatted per style guide
- [ ] Acronyms defined on first use

- [ ] Follows sentence case for headings
- [ ] Uses preferred terminology

- [ ] Clear introduction (problem + promise)
- [ ] Logical section flow
- [ ] Proper heading hierarchy (H1 → H2 → H3)
- [ ] Effective conclusion with CTA

**SEO**:
- [ ] Primary keyword in first 100 words and one H2
- [ ] Meta title (50-60 chars) and description (150-160 chars)
- [ ] URL slug optimized (lowercase, hyphens, keyword)
- [ ] Images have descriptive alt text

**Quality**:
- [ ] Specs and certifications factually accurate
- [ ] No unsubstantiated claims (every claim needs proof)
- [ ] Buyer's question is answered ("Why WOWOHCOOL?")
- [ ] Clear next step for the reader
