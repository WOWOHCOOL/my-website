### Battery Shipping Regulations (2026)
- **IATA DGR 67th Edition** (effective 2026-01-01): SOC ≤ 30% mandatory for lithium battery air shipments
- **IMDG Code 42-24**: SOC ≤ 30% for sea freight
- **UN3480**: Lithium batteries **alone** — applies to standalone power banks, spare cells, and external battery packs (Class 9 dangerous goods, stricter rules)
- **UN3481**: Lithium batteries **packed with or contained in equipment** — only applies when the battery is integral to a device (e.g., phone, laptop, wireless charger with built-in battery)
