## 4. Quality Control Process

### 4-Stage QC (IQC → IPQC → FQC → OQC)

| Stage | Name | What We Check |
|:-----:|------|---------------|
| 1 | **IQC** (Incoming Quality Control) | Raw materials: cells, IC chips, PCBs, connectors, casing |
| 2 | **IPQC** (In-Process Quality Control) | Assembly line: soldering, component placement, wiring |
| 3 | **FQC** (Final Quality Control) | Finished product: charging performance, port function, button feel, LED indicators |
| 4 | **OQC** (Outgoing Quality Control) | Pre-shipment: packaging integrity, accessory completeness, labeling accuracy |

### Aging Test (Burn-in)
- **Duration**: 4 hours minimum per unit
- **Coverage**: 100% of units (not batch sampling)
- **Test**: Full charge-discharge cycle under load
- **Industry Standard**: Most factories do 2 hours or batch sampling only

### GaN Technology Comparison (WOWOHCOOL GaN V)

| Metric | Silicon (Traditional) | GaN 3 (Gen 3) | GaN V (Gen 5) |
|--------|:---------------------:|:------------:|:------------:|
| Efficiency | ~85% | 90-92% | **93-95%** |
| Size vs Silicon | Baseline | 50% smaller | **60% smaller** |
| Heat Dissipation | Baseline | 20% better | **30% better** |
| Switching Frequency | ~100 kHz | ~500 kHz | **~1 MHz** |
| Lifespan (cycles) | ~500 | ~1,200 | **~1,500** |
| Bandgap | 1.12 eV | 3.4 eV | 3.4 eV |

### PD 3.1 Power Delivery
- USB-IF standard: supports up to **240W** (48V @ 5A)
- Fixed voltages: 5V, 9V, 15V, 20V
- Adjustable voltage (EPR): up to 48V
- Backward compatible with PD 2.0/3.0
- PPS (Programmable Power Supply) for Samsung/Google fast charging

### Qi2 Wireless Charging
- Qi2 MPP (Magnetic Power Profile): 15W universal
- N52H neodymium magnets (strongest grade commercially available)
- Qi2 v2.2.1 (July 2025): 25W extended power profile
- Backward compatible with Qi 1.3.x

### Semi-Solid-State Battery
- Electrolyte: Gel-based (not liquid) — no leakage risk
- Energy Density: 30% higher than Li-Polymer
- Thickness: 50% thinner profile
- Thermal Runaway Risk: Significantly reduced vs. liquid electrolyte
- Cycle Life: 500+ cycles to 80% capacity
- Featured at CES 2026

### USB-C PD 3.1 Adoption Rates
- 65% of new wall chargers now USB-C PD (2026)
- 60-70% of in-use chargers still old standard — multi-billion replacement opportunity
- EU Common Charger Directive: USB-C mandatory for 13 device categories (Dec 2024), laptops (Apr 2026)
